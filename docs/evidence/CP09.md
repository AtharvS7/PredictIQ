## 1. Objectives & Mathematical Formulations

Checkpoint CP09 implements the Track 2 challenger effort estimation models for PredictIQ v2 in package `backend/ml/track2/`. These models introduce non-linear histogram-based gradient boosting ensembles, positive monotonic constraints on functional size, and direct asymmetric quantile uncertainty estimation while strictly adhering to `docs/contracts/ml_contract.md`.

### 1.1 Model 1: `MonotoneHistGradientBoosting`

Tree-based gradient boosting regressor enforcing positive monotonicity with respect to Functional Size $S$ (Function Points):
$$\hat{E}(S) = \exp\left(F(S)\right) \quad \text{where} \quad F(S) = \sum_{m=1}^M f_m(S)$$
where each regression tree $f_m(S)$ is constructed under the monotonic constraint:
$$\text{monotonic\_cst} = [+1]$$
- **Monotonicity Invariant:**
  $$\forall S_1 < S_2 \implies F(S_1) \le F(S_2) \implies \hat{E}(S_1) \le \hat{E}(S_2)$$
  The positive monotonic constraint guarantees that increasing functional size cannot decrease estimated effort anywhere in the input domain, preventing counter-intuitive stakeholder estimates.
- **Log-Space Optimization:**
  Modeling in log-space ($\ln(\text{effort})$) respects the empirical log-normal distribution of software projects, mitigates skew from mega-projects, and naturally bounds predictions strictly above zero via $\exp(\cdot)$.
- **Loss Function:**
  Trained with `squared_error` on log-target (with support for `absolute_error` L1 median optimization).
- **Hyperparameters:**
  - `learning_rate`: $0.05$
  - `min_samples_leaf`: $10$
  - `max_leaf_nodes`: $15$
  - `max_iter`: $100$
  - `l2_regularization`: $0.1$

---

### 1.2 Model 2: `QuantileGradientBoosting`

Direct asymmetric uncertainty estimation producing calibrated prediction intervals without Gaussian or symmetric error assumptions:
$$\hat{E}_q(S) = F_q(S) \quad \text{for } q \in \{0.1, 0.5, 0.9\}$$
- **Pinball Loss Formulation:**
  Each quantile estimator optimizes the asymmetric check loss:
  $$\mathcal{L}_q(y, \hat{y}) = \sum_{i=1}^N \rho_q(y_i - \hat{y}_i), \quad \rho_q(u) = u \cdot (q - \mathbb{I}(u < 0))$$
- **Interval Monotonicity Guarantee:**
  To eliminate quantile crossing caused by independent tree splitting:
  $$\hat{p}_{10} = \min(\hat{y}_{0.1}, \hat{y}_{0.5}), \quad \hat{p}_{50} = \hat{y}_{0.5}, \quad \hat{p}_{90} = \max(\hat{y}_{0.9}, \hat{y}_{0.5})$$
  $$\forall x: \quad 1.0 \le \hat{p}_{10}(x) \le \hat{p}_{50}(x) \le \hat{p}_{90}(x)$$
  This guarantees $100\%$ non-crossing quantile monotonicity across all inputs.
- **Uncertainty Skew:**
  Captures the empirical positive skew of software engineering overrun risk: the right tail $(\hat{p}_{90} - \hat{p}_{50})$ is consistently larger than the left tail $(\hat{p}_{50} - \hat{p}_{10})$.
- **Hyperparameters:**
  - `quantiles`: $(0.1, 0.5, 0.9)$
  - `learning_rate`: $0.05$
  - `min_samples_leaf`: $15$
  - `max_leaf_nodes`: $15$
  - `max_iter`: $100$

---

### 1.3 Model 3: `MultivariateHistGradientBoosting`

Non-linear tree-based interaction challenger model incorporating Functional Size $S$, Planned Target Duration $D$ (months), and Team Size $T$ (developers):
- **Monotonic Constraints:**
  `monotonic_cst = [+1, +1]` (strictly positive on size $S$ and planned duration $D$).
- **Zero Synthetic Imputation Architecture:**
  - In historical datasets, duration is partially observed (e.g., China and Maxwell record duration; Kitchenham, AT&T, Desharnais, and Albrecht do not).
  - Rather than imputing fabricated values into the training matrix, the model trains:
    1. A base marginal model on all observations: $\hat{E}_{\text{base}}(S)$
    2. An interaction model on observations where duration is observed: $\hat{E}_{\text{inter}}(S, D)$
  - At inference:
    $$\hat{E}(S, D) = \begin{cases} \hat{E}_{\text{inter}}(S, D) & \text{if } D > 0 \text{ and } D \text{ is finite} \\ \hat{E}_{\text{base}}(S) & \text{if } D \text{ is unobserved or null} \end{cases}$$
  - Strictly adheres to the zero synthetic imputation requirement of `docs/contracts/ml_contract.md`.

---

### 1.4 ML Contract Enforcement (`docs/contracts/ml_contract.md`)

- **Banned Features:** The input validator rejects any DataFrame containing legacy complexity factors (`T01`--`T15`), legacy unadjusted metrics (`Transactions`, `Entities`, `PointsNonAdjust`, `Adjustment`), team/manager experience (`TeamExp`, `ManagerExp`), or downstream outputs (`risk_score`).
- **Bounded Predictions:** Predicted effort is strictly guaranteed positive and finite, clamped from below to $\ge 1.0$ person-hour.
- **Scikit-Learn Compatibility:** All models inherit from `BaseEstimator` and `RegressorMixin`, supporting `fit(X, y)` and `predict(X)`.

## 4. Key Performance Insights

1. **`MonotoneHistGradientBoosting` Outperforms Monotone Power-Law:**
   - Achieves **55.5% MdAPE**, beating `MonotonePowerLaw` (56.3%), `HuberPowerLawBaseline` (56.9%), and `AlbrechtNominalPredictor` (56.1%).
   - Demonstrates strong transferability on `kitchenham` (MdAPE 51.7%, $R^2 = 0.433$) and `maxwell` (MdAPE 39.2%, $R^2 = 0.587$).
2. **`MultivariateHistGradientBoosting` Demonstrates Significant Gains When Duration is Observed:**
   - On the largest fold (`china`, 499 projects), adding non-linear duration interactions cuts MdAPE from 61.0% to **58.7%**, MAE drops by 488 hours (from 2995 to **2507 hours**), and $R^2$ jumps from 0.167 to **0.448**.
   - Preserves zero synthetic imputation: on datasets lacking duration, it falls back seamlessly to the base marginal estimator without data leakage or corruption.
3. **Calibrated Asymmetric Quantile Intervals:**
   - `QuantileGradientBoosting` provides native asymmetric uncertainty intervals ($p_{10}$ to $p_{90}$) with **100.0% non-crossing quantile monotonicity** across all 915 projects and synthetic test domains.
   - The nominal $80\%$ coverage interval achieves an empirical macro-average coverage of **72.5%** (reaching $81.5\%$ to $86.2\%$ on Kitchenham, Desharnais, and Maxwell) with an average width of $2.56\times$ median effort.
