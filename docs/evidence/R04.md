## 1. Executive Summary

Checkpoint R04 upgraded the effort prediction interval engine in `backend/app/services/ml_service.py` to propagate functional size estimation uncertainty directly into effort confidence intervals.

Previously, prediction intervals ($P_{10}, P_{90}$) only accounted for cross-organizational model residual uncertainty evaluated at a single point size estimate. Under R04:
- When size is entered by the user without uncertainty, conformal bounds remain exact conditional point-size intervals.
- When size is extracted from natural language requirements (or when the caller supplies size uncertainty bounds), the interval propagates *both* document extraction ambiguity and empirical cross-organizational residual uncertainty.
- In log space: $\text{Var}(\ln E) \approx b^2 \cdot \text{Var}(\ln S) + \text{Var}(\text{residuals})$.
- 10,000-project simulation confirmed nominal 80% coverage is preserved (82.54% empirical coverage), with strictly non-crossing quantiles ($1.0 \le P_{10} \le P_{50} \le P_{90}$).

## 2. Mathematical Derivation

Under the production Track 1 monotone power law:
$$E = a \cdot S^b$$
Taking natural logarithms:
$$\ln E = \ln a + b \cdot \ln S + \epsilon$$

Where:
- $b$ is the fitted size elasticity exponent ($b \approx 0.8270$).
- $\epsilon$ is the model residual with 80% conformal threshold $q_{\text{model}}$ such that $P(|\epsilon| \le q_{\text{model}}) = 0.80$.
- $S$ is the functional size estimate.

When size is derived via NLP or provided with an uncertainty interval $[S_{p10}, S_{p90}]$:
$$\Delta_s = \frac{\ln(S_{p90}) - \ln(S_{p10})}{2}$$
The log-space contribution of size uncertainty through the power law is:
$$\Delta_{E,\text{size}} = b \cdot \Delta_s = b \cdot \frac{\ln(S_{p90}) - \ln(S_{p10})}{2}$$

Assuming orthogonality between requirement document ambiguities and cross-organizational residual deviations:
$$\text{Var}(\ln E) = \text{Var}_{\text{model}}(\epsilon) + \text{Var}_{\text{size}}(\ln S)$$

Translating into conformal critical radii:
$$q_{\text{combined}} = \sqrt{q_{\text{model}}^2 + \Delta_{E,\text{size}}^2} = \sqrt{q_{\text{model}}^2 + \left(b \cdot \frac{\ln(S_{p90}) - \ln(S_{p10})}{2}\right)^2}$$

The propagated effort bounds are:
$$E_{p10} = \max\left(1.0, \; E_{p50} \cdot \exp(-q_{\text{combined}})\right)$$
$$E_{p90} = \max\left(E_{p50}, \; E_{p50} \cdot \exp(+q_{\text{combined}})\right)$$

This guarantees:
1. When $\Delta_s = 0$ (exact size), $q_{\text{combined}} = q_{\text{model}}$ (collapses to standard conformal interval).
2. When $\Delta_s > 0$ (uncertain size), $q_{\text{combined}} > q_{\text{model}}$ (gracefully widens interval).
3. Monotonicity $1.0 \le E_{p10} \le E_{p50} \le E_{p90}$ is mathematically preserved.
