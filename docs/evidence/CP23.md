## 1. Objectives & Executive Summary

Checkpoint CP23 solidifies user trust, mathematical transparency, and data integrity for PredictIQ v2:
1. **Model Transparency & Data Provenance (`ModelTransparencyModal.tsx`):**
   - Implemented an accessible modal detailing the complete mathematical and empirical provenance of the v2 estimation engine.
   - Primary architecture: Track 1 `MultivariateMonotoneModel` ($E = a \cdot S^b \cdot D^c$) and `MonotonePowerLaw` ($E = a \cdot S^b$) selected under the frozen CP10 Decision Rule.
   - Anti-fabrication policy: Formal statement explaining the permanent elimination and prohibition of legacy v1 synthetic `T01`–`T15` complexity multipliers, uncalibrated adjustment tables, and the discredited 89.5% $R^2$ claim (caused by data leakage and circular target ratios).
   - Cited benchmark repositories: 6 gold-standard historical datasets totaling 915 curated software delivery projects: China (499), Kitchenham (145), AT&T (104), Desharnais (81), Maxwell (62), and Albrecht (24).
   - Uncertainty calibration: Split-conformal prediction intervals with 80% nominal empirical coverage (p10–p90) and Mondrian size bucketing (Small: < 100 FP, Medium: 100–500 FP, Large: > 500 FP).
   - Direct accessibility: Wired a prominent "How this estimate works" action button into `EstimationResultView.tsx` and `ResultsPage.tsx`, with clickable tier badge triggers.
2. **Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Guidance & Explanations (`OODDiagnosticsCard.tsx`):**
   - Created an expandable diagnostics container triggering whenever project parameters exceed empirical calibration envelopes (Functional Size > 2,000 FP, Team Size > 30, Target Duration > 24 months, Integration Density > 10).
   - Clearly explains why intervals widen for OOD projects without falsely clamping estimates: conformal residual dispersion naturally widens the p10–p90 interval while preserving monotonic power-law dynamics ($E = a \cdot S^b \cdot D^c$) rather than cutting off estimates with arbitrary ceilings.
   - Actionable engineering mitigations: scope decomposition, squad decoupling, and delivery milestone phasing.
3. **Export Flows & Contract Integrity (`exportContractUtils.ts`):**
   - Guaranteed strict adherence to `docs/contracts/api_estimate_v2.json`.
   - Deterministic transformation from internal `EstimateResult` models to the frozen v2 contract payload.
   - One-click "Copy JSON" action in `EstimationResultView.tsx`, `ResultsPage.tsx`, and `ModelTransparencyModal.tsx` copying the standardized schema payload directly to the clipboard with toast feedback.
4. **Rigorous Test Suite & Verification:**
   - 100% clean TypeScript typecheck (`npm --prefix frontend run typecheck`).
   - 100% passing Vitest suite across all 15 component test suites (74 tests) with `--pool=forks --no-file-parallelism`.
   - Flawless production build (`npm --prefix frontend run build`).

## 2. Deliverables & Component Architecture

### 2.1 Model Transparency Modal (`frontend/src/components/estimate/ModelTransparencyModal.tsx`)
- **Modal Container & Accessibility:**
  - Semantic ARIA attributes: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-labelledby="model-transparency-title"`.
  - Keyboard accessibility: pressing `Escape` closes the modal; clicking the backdrop dismisses the dialog.
  - Body scroll lock during modal visibility preventing background page movement.
- **Section 1: Primary Model Architecture (Track 1 Monotone Formulations):**
  - Displays mathematical formulations for `MultivariateMonotoneModel` ($E = a \cdot S^b \cdot D^c$) and `MonotonePowerLaw` ($E = a \cdot S^b$).
  - Explains the economic rationale: monotonic exponent constraint ($b > 0$), log-log linear formulation, and Huber robust loss fitting to resist heavy-tailed software project outliers.
- **Section 2: Elimination of Fabricated Features:**
  - Formal declaration of the ban on legacy synthetic `T01`–`T15` General System Characteristics.
  - Transparent debunking of the synthetic 89.5% $R^2$ claim, contrasting it with honest Leave-One-Source-Out (LOSO) cross-validation.
  - Enforced input policy: only user-declared parameters (Team Size, Planned Target Duration) and verified functional sizing (IFPUG 4.3.1 FP) are admitted to the inference pipeline.
- **Section 3: Cited Empirical Benchmark Repositories (915 Projects):**
  | Repository Source | Projects | Domain & Characteristics |
  |---|---|---|
  | **China** | 499 | Cross-industry software development (banking, government, manufacturing) |
  | **Kitchenham** | 145 | Telecommunications and high-reliability industrial software systems |
  | **AT&T** | 104 | Enterprise communications infrastructure and large-scale engineering |
  | **Desharnais** | 81 | Commercial information systems & Canadian financial applications |
  | **Maxwell** | 62 | European aerospace, defense, and complex commercial platforms |
  | **Albrecht** | 24 | Foundational IBM DP services benchmark establishing Function Point analysis |
- **Section 4: Split-Conformal Prediction & Mondrian Bucketing:**
  - 80% nominal empirical coverage guarantee (p10 optimistic lower bound to p90 conservative cap).
  - Mondrian size partitioning: independent non-conformity calibration across Small (< 100 FP), Medium (100–500 FP), and Large (> 500 FP) buckets to ensure localized variance calibration.
- **Section 5: Interactive Contract Inspection & Clipboard Export:**
  - Embedded contract inspector allowing users to preview and copy the full JSON contract payload adhering to `docs/contracts/api_estimate_v2.json`.

### 2.2 Expandable OOD Diagnostics Card (`frontend/src/components/estimate/OODDiagnosticsCard.tsx`)
- Replaces static warning text with an interactive, startup-grade diagnostic card.
- **Summary Header:**
  - Warning styling (`amber-50` / `dark:amber-950/30`), `AlertTriangle` icon, and `Statistical Variance Widened` badge.
  - Expand/collapse toggle button ("View Diagnostics & Why Intervals Widen" / "Hide Diagnostics") with `aria-expanded` support.
- **Detailed Diagnostics Panel:**
  1. *Triggered Envelope Exceedances:* Identifies specific parameter breaches (e.g. `Functional size of 3,500 FP exceeds standard calibrated envelope (max 2,000 FP)` or `Planned team size of 45 exceeds single-system envelope (max 30)`).
  2. *Statistical Mechanics:* Explains why intervals widen without arbitrary clamping: conformal residual dispersion naturally expands the quantile spread ($p10$ to $p90$) to preserve 80% coverage under extrapolation, while $p50$ continues to scale monotonically along the power-law curve.
  3. *Engineering Mitigations:* Specific advice on scope decomposition into iterative releases, squad decoupling into two-pizza teams, and schedule horizon phasing.

### 2.3 Contract Adherence & Export Utilities (`frontend/src/components/estimate/exportContractUtils.ts`)
- **`formatEstimateToV2Contract(estimate, currencyCode, currencyRate)`:**
  - Validates and normalizes `estimate_id` into standard RFC 4122 UUID format (`^[0-9a-f]{8}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{4}-[0-9a-f]{12}$`).
  - Formats effort quantiles ($p10$, $p50$, $p90$) in hours and person-months at the standard 152 hrs/PM rate.
  - Converts cost quantiles to the active currency with explicit 3-letter currency code and hourly rate.
  - Formats functional size with calibrated uncertainty bounds ($\pm 15\%$).
  - Emits structured estimation drivers (`Functional Size`, `Integration Complexity`, `Team Coordination Scale`, `Modern Architecture Stack`).
  - Emits standard assumptions (`Sizing`, `Effort Conversion`, `Hourly Rate`, `Uncertainty Calibration`).
  - Sets data quality tier (`A`, `B`, or `C`) and OOD flags.
  - Emits model metadata including version, Git/artifact SHA-256 hash (`0bea59fbe69a5b17acdf9953d61e659b26d3450052fd8934b2b1e0b716e0b257`), training row count (915), empirical sources, and validation metrics reference.
- **`validateV2Contract(payload)`:**
  - Validates full runtime JSON payloads against the JSON Schema defined in `docs/contracts/api_estimate_v2.json`.
