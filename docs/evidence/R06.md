## 1. Executive Summary

In Checkpoint R06, we performed a systematic audit of `docs/data/sources.yaml`, the canonical repository dataset `backend/ml/data/projects_v2.csv`, and surveyed potential candidate datasets from software engineering literature (including Tukutuku, SEERA, Kemerer, Finnish, and the COSMIC JSS 2025 literature compilation).

### Core Principle & Quality Bar
Every candidate source was evaluated against three non-negotiable criteria:
1. **Grain & Lifecycle Integrity:** Whole-project total development effort (not task, issue, commit, or sprint grain).
2. **Unit Integrity:** Standardized functional sizing (IFPUG 4.x Adjusted/Unadjusted Function Points or ISO/IEC 19761 COSMIC Functional Size Units). Zero synthetic conversion factors, gearing ratios, or fabricated inputs.
3. **Licensing & Provenance:** Documented open academic/public domain license with reproducible source files and verifiable SHA-256 hashes.
