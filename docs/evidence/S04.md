## 1. Executive Summary & Pre-Registered Criteria

Under frozen evaluation protocol `docs/contracts/evaluation_protocol_rc3.md`, PredictIQ evaluates challenger candidate models against the incumbent production estimator (`Model A: MonotonePowerLaw`) under strict 6-fold Leave-One-Source-Out (LOSO) cross-validation across all 915 records in `projects_v2.csv`.

To replace the incumbent production model, a challenger must meet **all four frozen decision criteria**:
1. **Criterion 1 (Macro MdAPE Superiority):** Relative MdAPE improvement $\ge 3.0\%$ across held-out out-of-domain folds.
2. **Criterion 2 (Non-Inferior PRED25):** Held-out PRED(25) remains within $\pm 1.0\%$ or improves.
3. **Criterion 3 (Monotonicity Guarantee):** Strictly monotonic with respect to functional size ($\partial E / \partial S \ge 0$).
4. **Criterion 4 (Input Integrity):** Zero requirement for unobservable, unverified, or synthetic complexity factors.

## 3. Pre-Registered Decision Attestation

- **Model B (Hierarchical Mixed-Effects):** While statistically significant in residuals ($p < 10^{-16}$), the out-of-source marginal population expectation yields an empirical Macro MdAPE of 56.14%, representing only a **0.29% relative gain**, well below the pre-registered 3.0% threshold. Furthermore, held-out PRED(25) dropped from 20.21% to 19.60%.
- **Model D (Quantile Regression):** Achieved Macro MdAPE of 57.84%, regressing by 2.72% relative to incumbent.
- **Track 2 (MonotoneHistGradientBoosting):** Achieved Macro MdAPE of 56.49%, showing no transfer generalization benefit over the parametric power law.

**Pre-Registered Decision:**
None of the challenger models met the pre-registered decision criteria. **Model A (`MonotonePowerLaw + MondrianConformal`) is unanimously confirmed and retained as the production serving estimator.**
